These links are optional and informational. ReleaseFence does not require, bundle, endorse, or exchange data with any other listed tool. Each project has an independent scope, lifecycle, security posture, and installation path.

- [mcp-client-autopsy](https://github.com/akigogikar/mcp-client-autopsy) — protocol-client diagnostics and reproducible evidence.
- [directivegraph](https://github.com/akigogikar/directivegraph) — directive relationship and conflict analysis.

Availability of one project says nothing about the readiness or support status of another.
